Bliss Blank

Voice Actor · Co-Director · Editor · Archivist

Bliss Blank is one of the defining voices and creative forces of Neural Nets and Pretty Patterns. As co-director, editorial guru, performer, and archivist, she has helped shape the sound and personality of Deep Dream State across its evolving arcs.

Her range is formidable. In Interrogation, Bliss Blank delivered a performance built around pressure, vulnerability, and psychological intensity. In Belle, she turned inward for an intimate portrait of monster romance. As Stephanie Ravenscroft, she created a submissive character with unusual emotional detail and specificity. Her performances combine technical control with a willingness to go somewhere genuinely strange, exposed, or difficult when a role requires it.

Within Deep Dream State, Bliss has become one of the show's central recurring performers. She plays Olivia in Chthonic, April in Incognitoh, and Dr. June Lowell through The Sitri Center and Vale Four. June in particular has grown into one of the major connective characters in the larger Neuralverse, moving between institutional authority, conspiracy, performance, and resistance.

Bliss is also an architect and archivist behind the scenes. She has appeared across hundreds of NNPP productions while helping organize and classify the enormous Neural Nets and Pretty Patterns recording library. Her work helps keep a project built across years, genres, series, and continuities navigable.

Every Saturday night at 9 PM Eastern, she hosts the Deep Drop Party , the long-running NNPP community listening event. The event has become its own weekly ritual, combining live listening, performance culture, conversation, and the sprawling history of the Neuralverse.

Across that larger body of work, Bliss Blank's voice forms part of its connective tissue. She has played scientists, sailors, authority figures, submissives, dreamers, and characters who become much harder to categorize. Whether the setting is psychological horror, speculative erotica, surreal science fiction, or monster romance, her performances bring the same mixture of precision, authority, and vulnerability.
