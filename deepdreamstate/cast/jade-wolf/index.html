Jade

Voice Actor · Emotional Support Werewolf™ · Hypnosis · ASMR · Auralism

Jade calls herself an Emotional Support Werewolf™, and the name fits.

She’s a primal critter with an affinity for cuddles and combat, blessed with stamina, fortitude, and a love for just about every kind of touch: smacks, boops, scritches, chomps, hugs, and snuggles.

Switchy by nature, she thrives on both sides of impact play. That extends to her voice acting as well as her kink resume. As an audio nerd, Jade is a devoted fan of hypnosis, ASMR, and auralism, lending her voice to dozens of projects. That voice, capable of shifting from casual chatter to grounded, resonant performance, has become one of her most distinctive tools.

In Deep Dream State, Jade has become one of the show’s shapeshifters. She moves between grounded narration and uncanny dream logic with ease. Her range lets her embody characters caught between comfort and collapse.

Listeners who enter the dream will hear that bite in every sense of the word: playful, dangerous, unforgettable.
