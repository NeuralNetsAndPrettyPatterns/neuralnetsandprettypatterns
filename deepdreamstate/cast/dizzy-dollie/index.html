Dizzy Dollie

Voice Actor · Glitch · Glam · Grit

Dizzy Dollie is a recurring voice actor in Deep Dream State and part of the wider Neural Nets And Pretty Patterns ensemble. Her performances bring a distinctive mix of glam, grit, instability, and momentum to characters who often seem to be dancing directly on the wire between control and collapse.

Her work fits especially well in the show's glitchier spaces, where dreams, systems, identities, and performances stop behaving cleanly. Dizzy Dollie's characters often feel like a pulse inside the static: vivid enough to command attention, but never entirely separable from the machinery around them.

Her Deep Dream State roles include Holly, Coven Leader, and Nyra. Her presence expands substantially across The Sitri Center and Vale Four, where the show's interest in performance, manipulation, and unstable reality becomes increasingly central.

That combination makes her especially effective in the Neuralverse. Dizzy Dollie's performances do not simply decorate the surrealism. They help make it feel inhabited, bodily, and immediate.
