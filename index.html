Who is Neural Nets And Pretty Patterns?

Writer, performer, and audio producer

Neural Nets And Pretty Patterns is a writer, performer, and audio producer working at the intersection of speculative fiction, immersive sound design, and monster romance.

The work keeps coming back to one question: what happens when you want something you're not supposed to, and who is set up to profit from that wanting? For years the answer was horror. Lately it's romance.

Neural Nets And Pretty Patterns originated the synthesized haptic narrative, which uses physical sensation, synced to the audio, as a deliberate storytelling layer. It was the first implementation of its kind in audio production.

His main body of work is the Neuralverse, a shared universe of audio drama, electronic music, experimental writing, and haptic pieces.

Deep Dream State

The flagship show of the Neuralverse: a full-cast monster romance and science fiction romance audio drama.

Seasons One through Four were desire horror, the genre the show started, where the scary part is how much you want the monster. Each arc was built on a real phenomenon: facial telemetry, dream research, reality TV surveillance, advertising technology.

Season Five, After After, flips it. Humanity has lost. Naia, a dryad group mind, gets to pick its replacement, and each season a different monster faction pitches her a love story. Listeners vote on which one wins.

A weekly live broadcast on Discord, every Saturday at 9 PM Eastern, running since February 23, 2018. Bliss Blank hosts, with a rotating cast of guest co-hostesses.

Each episode is a live set from the Neuralverse: devotional audio, haptic narrative, and storylines that run for months or years. It's free, and the archive lists every tracklist.
